Captain's Log

Welcome to Galaxy FM.

This is where I share my DJ mixes.

But these aren't traditional DJ sets.

I build each mix around a very specific idea. I might hear a particular bassline, drum pattern, sound, lyric, mood, or musical trick in a song — and then I go searching for other songs that connect to it. Eventually, those connections become an entire set.

See if you can figure out the theme.

Each set is collected into its own solar system, with each mix represented by a planet. Explore a planet, put on your headphones, and the music will reveal itself.

Headphones mandatory.

And Galaxy FM is a living thing.

Every planet is connected to a virtual hard drive sitting on a laptop in my basement studio. Whenever I make a new mix and save the file, it automatically finds its way into the galaxy.

More mixes.

More planets.

A bigger galaxy.

Pick a planet. Press play. See where it takes you.
